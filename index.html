Nick-Name:

My nick-name is Decagon and I use variations of it for most of my account names online. The origin of this name is from when my class was learning polygons in math or something. The teacher got to the decagon and people noticed the similarity to my name. Some people have referred to me as Decagon since that day. People don't use it that much, but there was one kid from my old school (at the time of this event it was my current school) who nearly exclusively called me Decagon. I haven't seen him in a bit but he was a kind person and I like the story this has given me along with allowing me to easily create usernames.
